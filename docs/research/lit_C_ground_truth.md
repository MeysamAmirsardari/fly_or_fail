## 0. Scope, limits, tag legend

These labels are the only validation set not built from the connectome weight matrix W. Are they large and balanced enough to test the model, and are they good enough to test structural predictors?

1. Literature access was abstract and snippet level only. Full texts (eLife, Nature, PMC, bioRxiv) were not readable. Per-neuron effect sizes, n, genotypes, hunger protocols and sucrose concentrations are mostly NOT verified.

## 2. Dose-response under silencing (hypothesis H-B)

**Bottom line: I found no published study that reports a PER (or ingestion) vs sucrose-concentration curve with and without silencing a circuit neuron and states whether silencing shifted the EC50 or reduced the maximum. H-B (threshold-margin redundancy) is therefore UNTESTED by the retrievable literature, and no published dose-response labels exist to validate a "shift vs abolition" classification.** This reflects only what abstracts and snippets could show; some such curves may exist in figure panels I could not read.

What the retrievable literature does provide:

| Finding | Relevance to H-B | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| Shiu 2022: individual silencing of 6 of 7 second-order types reduces PER, but silencing of third-order and premotor types does not, tested at a single concentration (50 mM) in food-deprived flies | Pattern is what a parallel-redundancy model predicts at one operating point (downstream layers have margin). But with one concentration and one hunger state it cannot distinguish "redundant" from "abolished at another operating point". A direct H-B test would repeat the silencing at lower concentrations (for example 5-10 mM) or shorter hunger | `[V-abs:W]` https://elifesciences.org/articles/79887/figures |
| Kim 2017: TPN activation influences feeding, inhibition has little effect ("parallel pathways") | Another redundancy example, again at undescribed concentrations | `[V-abs:C]` https://consensus.app/papers/details/de0e58c7655f55ed9044f532483a8e97/ |
| Marella 2012: TH-GAL4 > Kir2.1 lowered PER and the reduction "held across a range of sucrose concentrations"; flies still discriminated concentrations but were less sensitive at high concentrations (WebSearch paraphrase) | Most direct dose-response-under-silencing evidence found. Wording suggests a reduction of maximal response (gain loss) rather than a pure shift, but the primary figure was not read. Neurons are modulatory (dopaminergic), not path neurons | `[V-abs:W]` https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3310174 |
| Hunger state shifts the PER dose-response curve leftward (starved vs fed): "increased sugar sensitivity, seen as a leftward shift in the PER dose-response curve" (Inagaki et al. 2012 Cell, via Inagaki, Panse & Anderson) | Shows PER vs concentration curves are routinely measured and that an internal-state manipulation produces a SHIFT. State is a model operating-point analogue (cf. Poisson rate). Shiu 2022 says hunger acts on sensory and second-order neurons | `[V-abs:W]` https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3295637 |
| Gr5a-GAL4 > Kir2.1: reduced PER probability but no change in sucrose preference or consumption in choice assays | Phenotype depends on the readout threshold: partial silencing can hide in a choice or intake assay and show in PER. Supports the claim that a scalar necessity label is operating-point dependent | `[V-abs:W]` https://flybase.org/reports/FBal0155850 |
| Dahanukar et al. 2007: GRN dose-response curves for different sugars differ in threshold, slope and maximal firing | Sensory-level dose-response exists. No behavioral PER curves for mutants were found | `[V-abs:W]` https://www.vislab.ucr.edu/IGERT/papers/2007%20Dahanukar%20et%20al.pdf |
| Shiu 2024 in silico: silencing of top-200 neurons at 8 GRN rates (Fig 1F), water at 7 rates (Fig 4c) | The model itself yields shift-vs-abolition curves, but no experimental counterpart is available to compare | `[V-src]` figures.ipynb cells 11-14, 39-42 |

**Consequence.** For the project, H-B can be stated as a model prediction (and a proposed experiment), but it cannot be validated with existing public data. Do not promise an "EC50 shift vs abolition" validation unless a figure-level search of Shiu 2022 / Pool 2014 / Yapici 2016 supplementary data finds curves. A feasible indirect check is hunger-state: compare model "operating-point shift" to the known leftward shift under starvation.

**Multi-neuron (pairwise or combinatorial) silencing in flies.** I found NO study that silences two identified neuron types simultaneously in a sensorimotor circuit and reports interaction (epistasis). The one search designed to find such work returned only tools and a subset-vs-whole sleep comparison. The nearest evidence for non-additivity: Braun 2024 network co-activation requirement, von Reyn 2017 input-type removal, and Shiu 2022's observation that single third-order and premotor silencing has no effect (which implies, but does not test, synergy). H-C (neural epistasis) therefore has no published ground truth to validate against: `[V-abs:W]` for the searches, absence of evidence only. Prior-art checks for pairwise silencing in the Shiu model belong to the novelty agent.

## 5. Critical assessment

**Size.** About 8 named cell types (Rattle, Usnea, Clavicle, G2N-1, FMIn, Fdg, Bract, Roundup) have necessity labels that I can tie to pickle names, plus classes for the sensory endpoints and MN9. After merging bilateral pairs and removing conflicts (Fdg, FMIn), the clean binary set is roughly 6-10 types. Exact-test power with this size: a Mann-Whitney AUC (model necessity score vs label) must be at least 0.92 for p < 0.05 with 5 positives and 5 negatives, 0.86 with 6 and 6, 0.84 with 7 and 7, and 0.77 with 10 and 10 (my exact calculation; two-sided). So this set can reject only a very strong effect and cannot rank two predictors that differ modestly (for example betweenness vs flow). It is a held-out sanity check, not a benchmark.

**Balance.** There are both positives and negatives, which is unusually good, because Shiu 2022 and 2024 report negative results (third-order, premotor, 4 of 5 water-responsive neurons without a water phenotype). However (i) the counts per class are unknown without the full text; (ii) negatives are neurons already selected as on-path (see bias 1 below).

**Biases to carry explicitly.**
1. *Selection bias.* Tested neurons were chosen because they are on the sugar-to-MN9 path in the connectome and have split-GAL4 lines (Sterne 2021 produced 138 SEZ cell-type lines `[V-abs:C]` https://consensus.app/papers/details/11e6a00b94a656cfaa73648ba4a31f44/ ). The ground truth covers only the high-structure region. It cannot test whether global centrality fails off-path, because off-path negatives were not tested, and it compresses the dynamic range of path-based predictors (H-A).
2. *Positive-result and publication bias.* Papers report necessity for chosen cell types; weak or null results may be unreported. The Fdg conflict (necessary in Flood 2013 and Sakurai 2021 vs mixed in Shiu 2024) shows labels depend on lab, driver, and silencing mode.
3. *Genetic-line specificity.* Split-GAL4 lines (for example SS numbers in the search summaries) may label extra cells or only part of a type. A "no effect" can mean poor driver coverage, and a "reduced PER" can come from off-target cells. Bilateral silencing in experiments vs the unilateral input (right GRNs only) in the model.
4. *Chronic vs acute silencing.* Kir2.1, TNT, and ablation allow compensation; GtACR1 is acute but its efficacy is unmeasured here. Chronic Kir2.1 gave "no significant effects on PER modulation" in one Scott-lab study (summary only), which illustrates the gap `[V-abs:W]` https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9529979 .
5. *Operating point.* Hunger (food-deprived), a single sucrose concentration (50 or 100 mM), single optogenetic light intensity. The same neuron may be necessary at 10 mM and dispensable at 100 mM (H-B). Labels are valid at one point only.
6. *Different readouts.* PER (binary, per-trial) vs ingestion, choice preference, and feeding microstructure. A single neuron can give opposite sign labels across readouts (Gr5a > Kir2.1: PER down, preference unchanged; Rattle: PER reduced vs yeast feeding unchanged).
7. *Unmodelled biology.* The LIF model has chemical synapses only, with predicted neurotransmitter signs and a global weight scale. Electrical synapses (gap junctions), neuromodulators (dopamine, octopamine, peptides such as hugin, leucokinin, CCAP), hunger gating and peptidergic co-transmission (SELK) are absent, but experimentally shape labels (hunger acts at specific second-order neurons per Shiu 2022 `[V-abs:C]`). Disagreement between model and experiment can therefore reflect missing physiology, not wrong structure.
8. *Weak circularity guard.* Shiu 2024 reportedly set the single global weight so that 100 Hz GRN input gives about 80% of maximal MN9 firing (secondary source, Lacuna summary; unverified) `[V-abs:W]` https://lacuna.tiptreesystems.com/work/a-drosophila-computational-brain-model-reveals-sensorimotor-processing/wrk_d20d8a1222d35fae4df1aac0397998f9 . The same authors then compared to these experiments (reported "91% consistent over 164 predictions", secondary and unverified). The labels are therefore not blind to the model's authors, though they are independent of any structural predictor you will build.
9. *ID and convention drift.* v630 vs v783 root IDs, a left/right flip between notebooks and annotations, and heterogeneous GRN subclasses (section 1.3).
10. *Same-lab dominance.* Most PER necessity labels come from the Scott lab and collaborators (Shiu, Sterne, Kim, Pool, Gordon), so protocol effects correlate across labels.

**How to use it.**
- As a small, pre-registered, held-out validation set for H-E: rank-based (AUC or Spearman against an ordinal label: reduced / mixed / none), computed over cell types with bilateral pairs merged, with bootstrap CIs. Do not fit or tune any model on it. State up front that the plausible CI will be wide.
- Pre-register which labels count (for example only GtACR1 or acute data from Shiu 2022/2024) and treat conflicting types (Fdg, FMIn) as an uncertain stratum or as a sensitivity analysis.
- Use sufficiency (activation) labels separately from necessity labels. Many more exist (Shiu 2024 SEZ screen, Cande 2018, Kim 2017 etc.), and model activation-to-MN9 is the better-validated part of the model.
- Do not use the ground truth to claim a dose-response result (section 2) or epistasis (section 4).
- Treat the main computational contribution as the model-internal analysis (ladder H-A; operating-point theory H-B), with the experiments providing a modest external anchor. Consider internal replication (left/right homologs, v630 vs v783) for more power.
- To enlarge the set: the eLife source-data files for Shiu 2022 (not accessible from this container) should give per-genotype PER proportions and n. A user with browser access could extract them in an hour.
